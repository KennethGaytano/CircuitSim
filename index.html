      Steps and tips

        How to wire: drag from one terminal (circle) to another, or tap one then the other. Tap or drag a wire to remove it. When the circuit is complete, tap the switch or breaker to turn it on. On a keyboard, use Tab and Enter.

        The steps show one way to wire it. The simulator accepts circuits that meet the activity goal. This is a learning model, not a guide for real electrical installations.

        Click switch/breaker: toggle · Drag terminals: connect · Drag wire: remove · Drag elsewhere: rotate · Scroll/buttons: zoom
