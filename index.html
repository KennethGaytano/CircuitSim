CircuitSim
For first-year Industrial Art students

Build, test and fix circuits without touching a live wire

CircuitSim is a hands-on breadboard simulator. Drag parts into place, watch the current change, and practice finding faults safely.

Three skills, one simulator

Skill 1

Construction

Drag resistors, LEDs, switches and wires into a real breadboard layout.

Skill 2

Analysis

Predict the current with Ohm's law, then check it against the live reading.

Skill 3

Troubleshooting

Open a broken circuit, find the fault, and fix it.

How it works

1

Pick a challenge

Each challenge trains one skill.
2

Drag parts onto the board

Tap a part to flip an LED, toggle a switch, or change a resistor.
3

Get instant feedback

The simulator tells you what is happening and why.

Simulator
